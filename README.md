U.S. Grocery Price Inflation Analysis (2020–2025)

Project Overview

This project analyzes U.S. grocery price inflation from 2020 through 2025 using Consumer Price Index (CPI) data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS).

The project was completed as a capstone for the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate program.

Objective: Identify grocery inflation trends, determine which years experienced the highest inflation, and evaluate how grocery prices changed over the six-year period.

Data Source

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS)

Dataset: Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), Food at Home

Series ID: CUUR0000SAF11

Period: January 2020 – December 2025

Official BLS CPI Data

Key Findings

Highest inflation year: Grocery inflation reached approximately 11.42% in 2022.

Inflation slowdown: Annual grocery inflation declined to approximately 5.02% in 2023 and 1.19% in 2024.

Cumulative price increase: Grocery prices increased approximately 25.19% between 2020 and 2025.

Data limitation: October 2025 CPI data was unavailable because of the federal government shutdown. The 2025 annual average uses 11 available monthly observations.

Project Methodology

The analysis followed the six phases of the Google Data Analytics process:

Ask: Defined the research questions and project objectives.

Prepare: Collected publicly available CPI data.

Process: Reviewed, cleaned, and organized the dataset.

Analyze: Calculated annual averages and inflation rates.

Share: Created line and column charts to communicate trends.

Act: Developed conclusions and recommendations based on the findings.

Conclusion

U.S. grocery prices increased substantially between 2020 and 2025, with the largest annual inflation rate occurring in 2022.

Although inflation slowed in subsequent years, grocery prices remained significantly higher than their 2020 levels.
